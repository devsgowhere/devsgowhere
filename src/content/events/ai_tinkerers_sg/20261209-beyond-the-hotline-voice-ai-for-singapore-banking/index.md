A customer starts a bank account application and gets stuck at proof of address. They request a callback. A voice agent calls, guides them through the bank’s authentication step, and explains which document is missing. The customer opens the bank app to upload it. If they need a banker, the banker picks up with the context already in hand.

What would it take to make that experience work in Singapore?

Join fellow banking, fintech, and risk professionals for a meetup exploring three ways banks could use ElevenLabs voice agents:

-   **Help applicants finish KYC.** Follow up on an incomplete application, explain what’s outstanding, and guide the customer back to the bank’s secure channel.
    
-   **Keep loan applications moving.** Answer a borrower’s question while they have the application open, clarify the next step, or arrange a callback with a lending specialist.
    
-   **Resolve everyday support calls.** Help callers check payment status and, when someone flags an unfamiliar transaction, bring in the fraud team with the details already captured.
    

We’ll draw on Better’s mortgage assistant, Klarna’s frontline phone support, and Funding Societies’ financing outreach in Southeast Asia. Then we’ll tackle the question that matters for a Singapore rollout: **In a world of scam calls, how can a customer tell it’s really their bank calling?**

If you work in retail banking, lending, onboarding, contact centres, fraud, risk, or compliance, bring one point where your customers regularly get stuck. That’s where the discussion starts.
