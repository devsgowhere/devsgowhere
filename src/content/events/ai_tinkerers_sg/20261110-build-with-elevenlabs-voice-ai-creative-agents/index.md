**From idea to working prototype — in one evening.**

Ever wanted to build an AI agent that can actually talk to you? Or create realistic voices, sound effects, and AI-generated audio content?

Join us for a free, hands-on community **in-person workshop** where you’ll explore the latest voice AI capabilities from ElevenLabs and build something of your own.

No long presentations. No endless slides. Just learning by doing.

**Date:** Tuesday, 10 November 2026  
**Time:** 5:30pm - 9:00pm  
**Venue:** Grab HQ @ one-north

**Admission:** Free, registration required  
**Format:** In-person, hands-on build-along workshop

**What will you build?**

Choose your own adventure:

**Track 1: Build a Voice AI Agent**

Create a conversational AI agent that can listen, speak, and respond to questions using your own instructions and knowledge base.

**Track 2: Create with ElevenCreative**

Experiment with AI-generated voices, narration, music, and sound effects to produce your own short audio creation.

**What to expect**

-   Live demonstrations of ElevenAgents and ElevenCreative
    
-   Guided, step-by-step hands-on building
    
-   ElevenLabs credits for eligible registered participants, subject to confirmation
    
-   Opportunities to showcase your creation
    
-   Networking with fellow AI builders and creators
    

**Who should attend?**

Developers, AI enthusiasts, startup founders, students, content creators, and anyone curious about building with voice AI.

Beginners are welcome. Coding experience is helpful for advanced customisations but not required for the guided activities.

**What to bring**

Your laptop, headphones, curiosity, and an ElevenLabs account.

**Come curious. Leave with something you’ve built.**
