What have you been building? Join AI Tinkerers Singapore for an evening of live demos with Supabase and Temus.

We'll kick things off with an introduction to **Supabase** and what you can build with it, followed by a live demo from **Temus** showcasing a project built with OpenAI Codex and Supabase.

After that, the floor is yours. If you’ve got a prototype to share, put your name on the demo list while you grab some food. Show us what it does and tell us one thing you learned building it. Works in progress are welcome.

You can also come to watch, ask the Supabase engineers questions, and meet the people behind the demos.

**The evening, in Singapore time**

-   **5:30–6:00 pm** · Check-in
    
-   **6:00–6:30 pm** · Supabase introduction & demo from Temus
    
-   **6:30–7:00 pm** · Grab food and sign up to demo
    
-   **7:00–8:00 pm** · Community show & tell
    
-   **8:00–8:30 pm** · Wrap-up and networking
    

Thank you to Supabase for generously sponsoring the food and drinks, and for providing Supabase credits to every guest who checks in at the meetup.
