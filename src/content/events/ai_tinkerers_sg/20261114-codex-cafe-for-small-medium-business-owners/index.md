Codex Café Singapore ☕

Bring your laptop. Bring a business problem. Let’s build over coffee.

Join a small, curated group of 7–10 SME business owners for a relaxed, hands-on session exploring what you can build with Codex.

Got a spreadsheet you spend too long updating, a report you keep putting off, or an idea for a simple business tool? Bring it along. We’ll talk through your workflow, pick a manageable task, and start building together.

Expect a brief introduction to Codex, time to work on your own problem with guidance, and open conversations with other business owners. The aim is to leave with a small prototype or a clear next step you can try in your business.

What to bring

-   Your laptop and charger
    
-   One business problem you’d like to work on
    
-   Relevant sample data, with confidential or personal
