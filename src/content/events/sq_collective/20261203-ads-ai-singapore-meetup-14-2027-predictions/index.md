What will change in ads and AI in 2027? At **Singapore Ads & AI Meetup #14**, we’ll put four predictions up for debate with industry veterans and the audience. Bring a prediction you’re willing to defend.

-   **AI infrastructure.** Will the AI infrastructure bubble burst in 2027? What would that mean for the companies building on top of it?
    
-   **AI monetization and advertising.** Will Anthropic introduce ads? Could AI advertising reach $50 billion by the end of 2027? Where will DSPs and SSPs fit if AI platforms build their own ad businesses?
    
-   **Publishers and creators.** Can publishers grow without relying so heavily on Google? Which sell-side businesses are at risk, and how can publishers and creators keep making money as discovery shifts to AI?
    
-   **AI adoption and agents.** How many people will use a personal AI agent to compare products, make purchases, or handle everyday tasks? Could Google’s share of search fall below 50%?
    

This is a free, in-person meetup for founders, builders, marketers, publishers, operators, and anyone curious about what comes next. Everyone is welcome.
