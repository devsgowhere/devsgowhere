You already know you need a brand and you need to be posting. You've watched the videos and saved the threads. But you still haven't started.

Building : Brand & Content isn't another talk you sit through and forget. It's 3 hours of learning one step, then doing it straight away, together.

We'll share the content strategies used by creators like Emonee (900K+ followers) and Dan Koe (1M+ followers), adapted for B2C business owners, plus how to design a brand your customers will remember and trust.

**How it works**

Each step follows the same rhythm: a short lesson, a live demo, then you do it yourself while we go around helping. You'll work in small groups in a coworking space, surrounded by a like-minded community, giving each other feedback and ideas. No design background needed. We'll use AI (a paid ChatGPT or Claude plan) to help you move fast.

**What you'll do**

**Part 1: Understand how content works**

1. ******_Brands are built on vibes, not just logos._** Why the brands growing today are built on social, and why good aesthetics alone won't earn trust.  2. ******_How organic content works._** How Instagram, TikTok, Twitter/X and Threads work, why going viral doesn't mean getting customers, and how to pick the right platform for your audience.

**Part 2: Find your content**

3. ******_Research your niche._** Follow our step-by-step guide to study your niche on Instagram and save 20–50 videos that stand out.  4.** ****_Break it down._** Learn how almost all organic content comes down to hooks and formats, then break down what you saved.  5. **_Repurpose it for your business_**_._ Pick the 5–10 you can adapt, and turn them into your content bank.

**Part 3: Build your brand**

6. **_Define your values and voice._** What you stand for and how you sound, so everything you post feels like you.  7. ******_Build your visual language._** Study your competitors and choose colours and typography that either stand out in your space or fit in with it.  8. **_Create your starter brand kit with AI_**_._ Feed your research and values into AI to generate a logo, colours and typography you can use today and refine later.

**Part 4: Go live**

9. **_Set up your social profile._** Display photo, bio and cover image (if your platform uses one), all using your new brand kit. 10. **_Commit to your first post._** Pick your first post, set a date to publish it, and share it with the group for accountability.

**You'll leave with**

• A starter brand kit: values, voice, logo, colours and typography • A social profile that's set up and ready to post from • A content bank of 5–10 post ideas • A framework for researching your niche and breaking down why content works • The prompts and templates used in the workshop, to keep and reuse • Your first post planned, with a group holding you to it

**Who it's for**

B2C business owners selling their own physical or digital products or services, whether you're about to launch or already running and ready to take content seriously.

**Who it's not for**

Freelancers, or anyone building a personal brand to become an influencer or land sponsorships. This workshop is about growing a business.

**Before you come**

• Bring a laptop. • Have a paid ChatGPT or Claude plan ready. You'll need it to generate your starter brand kit.
