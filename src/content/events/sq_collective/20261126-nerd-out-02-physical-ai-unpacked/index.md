**Robots are folding laundry. AI gadgets are launching every week. What actually matters?** 🤖

Nerd Out is SuperNerds Club's monthly night for people who want to understand physical AI: the robots, the hardware and the market moving underneath them. It happens at SQ Collective on the last Thursday of every month. You get one evening, three sharp talks and one trivia game.

The program

🚪 **6:00 PM · Doors open + networking**  
👋 **6:30 PM · Meet SuperNerds Club**  
Who we are, why we started a physical AI community, and what's coming next.

📈 **01 · The month in Physical AI**  
🎧 **02 · Consumer Hardware Case Study**  
🦾 **03 · Robotics - Case Sharing**

🧠 **7:50 PM · 10-question trivia**  
Scan the QR code, play from your phone, beat the room. The winner takes home a mystery consumer AI device. 🎁 Questions may come from that night's talks, so pay attention.

🏆 **8:10 PM · Winner announced + networking till 9**

Who's it for?

Builders, founders, investors, operators and anyone who reads spec sheets for fun. You don't need a robotics PhD, just curiosity.

Good to know

-   It's free, but seats are limited. Every application is reviewed, so please fill in the questions properly.
    
-   No catering this time, so come fed.
    
-   Can't make it? There's another one next month, same time, same place: the last Thursday of every month.
