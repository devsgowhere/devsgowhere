The code is the easy part.  
FDE 101: My First 30 Days. Learn what the first month really looks like.

Learning to code is the part you train for. In a client-facing role, it turns out to be the easy part. The hard part is everything around it: understanding how a client’s business actually runs, learning systems you’ve never seen, and deciding what’s worth building before you build it. That’s the job of a Forward Deployed Engineer.

In this session, Wei Qi shares an honest look at his first 30 days as an FDE at Hasky. He went from university student and part-time barista to sitting in client meetings in his first week. He’ll cover the day 1 client call he didn’t understand, the feature he almost said yes to on the spot, and the five things that turned out to be harder than the code.

What you’ll hear

-   Knowing what’s going on: keeping up in client calls when you don’t understand yet, and how to close the gap fast
    
-   Talking to people: client meetings and networking as an introvert, and what changed after the first one
    
-   Learning the business: getting up to speed on ERPs, the software development life cycle and a client’s workflow in weeks
    
-   Deciding what to build: why “that’s easy, I can do that in hours” is the most dangerous thing to say to a client
    

What you’ll leave with

-   An honest picture of what the FDE role looks like in month one
    
-   Five lessons you can use in your own first month on the job
    
-   A simple rule for handling client requests before you commit
    

Who it’s for  
Students, fresh graduates, career-switchers and early-career engineers curious about forward deployed engineering, plus anyone who works with clients and wants a candid look at the job. You don’t need to be a senior developer. Every lesson comes from a real story.

About Wei Qi  
Wei Qi is a Forward Deployed Engineer at Hasky, building AI workflows for SMEs. Before Hasky, he was a university student and a part-time barista, which turned out to be good training for figuring out what clients actually want. In this session he’ll share what he learned in his first month, from client calls to first builds.

Part of Hasky’s Forward Deployment Academy. Bring your questions about the role. Leave knowing what the first month really looks like.
