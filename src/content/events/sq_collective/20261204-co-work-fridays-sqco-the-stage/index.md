## The Stage @ 1.30pm

**Title:**

**Speaker:**



---

Friday is our open co-working day. People come to work on their own projects, take breaks together, and usually stay through the afternoon. It's quiet, focused, and builder- and founder-led.

You can arrive anytime from 10AM. Most people show up after lunch and settle in before 1.30pm. Bring your laptop. Grab any open seat. Stay as long as you like.

At 1.30pm, we pause work briefly for a shared session called The Stage, then everyone goes back to working.

This is how Fridays here work.

## About SQ Collective

[SQ Collective](https://www.sq-collective.com/?utm_source=luma) is a co-working space in Singapore for founders and builders working with AI. Fridays are open co-working days, with a short shared session called The Stage at 1.30pm. People come to work, learn something practical, and go back to building.
