this is an interest check. sign up if you'd actually show up. leave your thoughts so we can shape this properly.

we're gauging interest before committing. if enough founders want this, we make it real.

**the format**

mondays, 10am to 6pm. co-work alongside other founders. trade notes, share war stories, help each other out.

communal lunch at 1.30pm. no sad desk lunches.

after lunch, someone shares. could be prepping for a raise, figuring out GTM, working through product decisions. recurring programming, not random one-offs.

every couple of months, we run a community-led co-founder matching session. facilitated by people who've actually been through it.

**the deal**

no external pressures. no sponsors shaping the agenda. no corporate partners picking the topics. just founders, building.

**why mondays**

we want to keep this communal. same people showing up, week after week. that's how you build habit. that's how you actually learn what someone is working on, not just what they say at an intro.

founders learn faster around other founders. you pool resources, you trade real notes.

no pressure to run things a certain way because someone wrote you a cheque. no board-driven timelines. no growth-at-all-costs defaults. just founders working at their own pace, on their own terms, with other people doing the same thing.

velocity of learning comes from repetition, not variety.
