**What is ProductTank?**
ProductTank is back! After a year away, we are restarting our meetups for product people with an evening of two talks, Q&A and time to catch up with the others in the Product community.

Whether you are a product manager, work closely with product teams, or are just curious about how scalable & delightful products get built, come along and join the conversation! RSVP to save your spot.

**Speakers Intro**

**[Dmitry Potapov](https://www.linkedin.com/in/dmitry-potapov-08412165/)**
Dmitry is Head of Product Operations at Foodpanda. He has over 10 years of product experience across e-commerce, food tech and platform solutions. In his talk, "Global Expansion: What Could Possibly Go Wrong?", he looks at the challenges product teams face when taking a product into new markets and what to plan for before expanding.

**[Kenneth Chang](https://www.linkedin.com/in/kennethchanglekloong)**
Kenneth Chang is a Senior Product Manager at FormSG, a product of Open Government Products. Users often use products in ways their makers never intended. Drawing on lessons from roguelike games, Kenneth shares unobvious learnings on accommodating these unusual use cases and building products that people love.
