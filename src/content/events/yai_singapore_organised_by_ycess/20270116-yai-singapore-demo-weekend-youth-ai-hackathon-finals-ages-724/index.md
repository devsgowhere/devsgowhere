- Organised by YCESS (Youth and Children Education Society Singapore), a society registered in Singapore.
- Teams of 1–4 build an AI project online in one of four quests: AI Art; AI Robotics & Smart Devices; AI for Good; AI Agents.
- Three divisions judged separately: Primary (7–12), Secondary (13–17), University (18–24, full-time students up to first-degree level).
- Registration closes Saturday 2 January 2027; projects are due Saturday 9 January 2027, 23:59 SGT.
- Demo Weekend in Singapore: Sunday 31 January 2027 (the finals, then the showcase and awards). Finalist teams based outside Singapore can present online.
- S$35,550 in prizes. S$68 per student, with fee-waiver places for students who need financial support. No coding needed; any AI tool is allowed.
- Exact times and venue to be confirmed; registration at https://yai.sg/register/?utm_source=list-devsgowhere&utm_medium=listing
