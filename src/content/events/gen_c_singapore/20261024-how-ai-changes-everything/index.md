🧱 **10.30am - 11.10am : From Noise to Data: Understanding Modern Generative Models**

This sharing session explores the fundamental ideas behind modern generative models through three perspectives: Diffusion, Score Matching, and Flow Matching. We will see how each approach transforms a simple distribution into a complex data distribution — by denoising, navigating, or transporting probability. We then connect these ideas to practical image-generation architectures, exploring why modern models often generate in latent space rather than directly in pixel space, how Variational Autoencoders (VAEs) provide compact and structured representations, and how Diffusion Transformers (DiT) use attention to model relationships within these representations.

About the Speaker:

🤖 **11.30am - 12.10pm : What Happens When Everyone Has AI?**

As AI tools become available to almost everyone, simply having access to them is no longer a competitive edge. Moving beyond prompts and demos, we will explore how AI is changing the way we work, compete and create value, including the productivity paradox (being faster doesn’t necessarily mean working less), what AI can and still can’t replace, and why judgment, context, domain expertise and human skills may matter more than ever. We will look at how AI could reshape jobs, teams and organisations, which skills will count when everyone has an AI assistant, and how individuals can still stand out in an AI-enabled world.
