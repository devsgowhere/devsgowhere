**Below is the schedule,**

🧱 **10.30am - 11.10am : Enabling More Sophisticated Retail Trading with Generative AI**

This session explores how generative AI can help retail traders cut through information overload, uncover insights from vast amounts of market data, test investment ideas, and make more informed decisions. We will quickly walk through how to get started with these tools, examine real-world applications and emerging capabilities, and discuss the limitations and challenges of using AI in financial markets.

About the Speaker: 

Sheng Jun Lim is passionate about the study of data and machine learning to improve decision making processes for financial markets. An engineer by training, he is an efficient and effective problem solver with a solid technical understanding of financial instruments and trading.

11.10am - 11.30am : Q&A session

🤖 **11.30am - 12.10pm : Getting Started with AI: Small Skills, Bigger Opportunities**

This talk is a beginner friendly, introductory session for anyone curious about AI. Attendees will learn how AI tools can help with job search, communication, learning, and everyday work tasks, while also understanding how to use these tools safely, confidently, and realistically. The session is designed to show that small new skills can create bigger opportunities over time.

About the Speaker:

Ray Goh is a Digital Transformation specialist responsible for digital strategy in Global Financial Markets at DBS Bank. Previously, he was also instrumental in leading the delivery of FX, Fixed Income Trading and Core Finance technology at the bank. A passionate Cloud advocate with a deep interest in Serverless services, Ray holds multiple AWS certifications.

12.10pm - 12.30am : Q&A session
