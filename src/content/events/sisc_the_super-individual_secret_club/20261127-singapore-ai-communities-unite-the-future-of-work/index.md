💎 **Panel 1 - Hire a Human or an Agent? Building the Next-Generation Workforce**

As AI agents take on more knowledge work, what happens to traditional hiring? Will companies still build large full-time teams? Where do fractional workers fit in? And perhaps most importantly — how does the next generation get its first job when entry-level work can increasingly be automated?

We'll bring together **executives, talent leaders, undergraduates, researchers, and contrarian thinkers** to debate what the next workforce actually looks like.

💎 **Panel 2 - From Nine-to-Five to Super Individual: Building a One-Person Unicorn**

A new generation of founders is building million-dollar businesses alone, powered by artificial intelligence agents rather than large teams.

Hear from solo founders who have done it, an investor backing this shift, a distribution expert, a researcher on technological disruption, and a founder who tried and failed. The opportunity is real, and it is moving fast.
