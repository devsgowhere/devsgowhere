**Course Overview**
This two-day interactive workshop introduces the practical foundations of effective product development in Scrum. Participants will explore how Developers work within a Scrum Team to build architecture and design incrementally, write tests before code, integrate continuously, and turn Product Backlog Items into usable Increments of value.
Through discussions and hands-on technical exercises, participants will experience how sound engineering practices enable teams to deliver working software in small, safe, frequent slices. We will also visit how AI-augmented development and Specifi cation-Driven Development are beginning to reshape these practices, setting the stage and preparing participants for where the craft is heading next.
The course is aligned with the Certifi ed Scrum Developer® (CSD®) learning objectives, but is offered independently as an uncertifi ed community learning initiative.
