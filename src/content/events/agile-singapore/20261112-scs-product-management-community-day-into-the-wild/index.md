The Singapore Computer Society Product Management SIG is hosting **Product Community Day – Into the Wild** on Thursday, 12 November, at 6pm at Lorong AI!

Organised by PDM SIG Chair and Agile Singapore member Regina Ong, SCS Product Community Day brings together a lineup of leading speakers and activities exploring emerging trends, insights and learnings from building digital products, platforms and services at the intersection of AI and humans.

Topics will also extend beyond product development to include areas such as regulating our nervous systems for high performance.

Leverage the collective experiences and take away new actionable approaches for navigating constraints and build your product edge with fellow product practitioners.

See you at the event!
