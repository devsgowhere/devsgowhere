AI agents are heading into the wild. Join us for an Agentic AI Night on November 19 at CapitaSpring in Singapore. We’ll dig into four new Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF) projects, what they do, where they fit, and how you could incorporate them into your own work. Bring your questions, your experiments, and your best “could an agent do this?” ideas.

[Vincent Caldeira](https://www.linkedin.com/in/caldeirav?utm_source=luma) will take us inside the Agentic AI Foundation, exploring its open standards, its place in the Linux Foundation ecosystem, and opportunities for builders in Singapore to get involved. Expect a closer look at how protocols such as MCP and A2A help agents use tools and delegate tasks, why openness matters for avoiding vendor lock-in, and how these pieces connect with cloud-native infrastructure and AI compute. He’ll also explore practical ways to contribute, from building regional MCP tools to shaping global specifications and putting Singapore’s Agentic AI Governance Framework into practice.

Come for the technical deep dive, stay for the lively conversations and a chance to swap ideas with the people building what comes next. Thanks to Red Hat for sponsoring the venue at its Level 45 office in CapitaSpring. See you up there!
