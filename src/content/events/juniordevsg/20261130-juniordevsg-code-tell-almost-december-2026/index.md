**Presentation**
**Topic: System thinking**

**Presenters:**
Michael Bowman, Senior Staff Software Engineer, PayPal
http://linkedin.com/in/bowmanmb
Satosh Jayamurugan, Senior Engineering Manager, Paypal
https://www.linkedin.com/in/santosh-jayamurugan-1066017/

Did your technical conversation become philosophical, or extend to seemingly unrelated factors? You may have been talking to a Systems Thinker. Systems Thinkers enable more insightful, sustainable, and adaptable approaches to problem-solving and decision-making in complex environments. And as a developer, you work in one every day.

Ever fixed a bug and watched it create two more? Shipped a feature that worked perfectly, then caused trouble somewhere nobody expected? That’s not bad luck. It’s a system at work.

Join PayPal’s own Mike Bowman (Architect) and Santosh Jayamurugan (Engineering Manager) for a 45-minute introduction to Systems Thinking. This isn’t a how-to course, and there’s no homework. You’ll leave with a new lens for looking at your code, your team, and your work, plus a few questions you’ll start asking that others in the room won’t.

**What you’ll get**

* A clear, jargon-free picture of what Systems Thinking is
* Examples from PayPal and life
* Why fixing symptoms rarely fixes problems
* A way of seeing the bigger picture that will set you apart early in your career

No prior knowledge needed. Only curiosity.
