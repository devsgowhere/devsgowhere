**Topic: Strongly Typed Terraform on AWS**
**Presenter:** Vincent De Smet, Platform Engineer, GoTymeX
https://www.linkedin.com/in/vincentds/

We will run through the AWS Workshop of TerraConstructs - it is a copy of the cdkworkshop but runs on opentofu using the CDKTF community fork "CDK Terrain" (which I helped create and maintain). TerraConstructs is a library of handcrafted (2024-2025) and LLM Assisted (2025-Today) port of AWSCDK to terraform-provider-aws. The workshop gives attendees a quick taste of deploying serverless applications on AWS without giving up control on the IaC State and execution. It elevates attendees understanding of what deterministic code (no LLM tokens needed) can provide over basic Terraform Modules.

No experience in AWS or Terraform is required, the prerequisites are listed on the workshop page: https://terraconstructs.dev/workshops/aws - but I will confirm if I can provide an environment that reduces the requirements on the candidate laptop and cloud provider. (to be confirmed)

**About Vincent**
Vincent De Smet started his DevOps and Platform engineering journey leading meetups for the Docker communities in Vietnam and Singapore in 2014, before moving and working for startups in Singapore from 2016 until 2021. While in Singapore Vincent helped organise CloudNative meetups and covered mostly Kubernetes and IaC on AWS topics. While working remotely since 2021, Vincent mainly focused on Platform Engineering and shifted more towards Serverless solutions and the CDK ecosystem. Currently Vincent leads initiatives around platform support for agentic software delivery at GoTymeX while also leading the community fork of CDK for Terraform at https://cdktn.io.

# About JuniorDevSG:

JuniorDevSG is a developer interest group that focuses on providing a place for junior developers to learn and network with each other.
At the same time, we seek to be a support system to help junior developers break into tech, navigate their careers as well growing them to one day become senior developers or tech leaders in the tech industry.
