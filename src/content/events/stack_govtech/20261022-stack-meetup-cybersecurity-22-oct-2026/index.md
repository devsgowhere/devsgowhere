Join us for the October edition of GovTech's STACK Meetup [Cybersecurity], featuring a series of lightning talks by industry experts and government practitioners. Explore how frontier AI is reshaping cyber risks, the rise of autonomous security operations, and the importance of strengthening identity resilience. We'll also examine why cybersecurity fundamentals remain critical in the age of AI, alongside developments in Operational Technology (OT) security. Join us for an evening of insightful sharing, engaging discussions, and networking with the cybersecurity community!

**Secure your spot for STACK Meetup [Cybersecurity] today: [https://go.gov.sg/stackmeetup-22oct2026-mc](https://go.gov.sg/stackmeetup-22oct2026-mc)**. Limited seats available!
