**We’re launching [Thoughtworks Pivot](https://www.thoughtworks.com/en-sg/careers/thoughtworks-pivot/singapore) in Singapore to help experienced women technologists who are a Singapore citizen or PR sharpen their skills, rebuild confidence and return to full-time software engineering after a career break. Learn more about Thoughtworks Pivot [here](https://www.thoughtworks.com/en-sg/careers/thoughtworks-pivot/singapore).**

**Join one of our Open House in November! Please RSVP via [this Google form](https://forms.gle/NrBPCyiMwEXMz3vY9).**
Meet the program team members and learn more about the program and selection criteria, and ask any questions you might have.

The Open House will be hosted at Thoughtworks Singapore office at 18 Cross St, #12-01 18 Cross, Singapore 048423. You can drop in anytime during 11:00 am - 12:00 pm on one of these dates:

* Friday, November 13, 2026
* Friday, November 20, 2026
* Friday, November 27, 2026

**Please RSVP via [this Google form](https://forms.gle/NrBPCyiMwEXMz3vY9).**
