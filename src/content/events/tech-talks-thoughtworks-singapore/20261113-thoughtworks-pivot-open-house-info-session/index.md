**We’re launching Thoughtworks Pivot in Singapore to help experienced women technologists sharpen their skills, rebuild confidence and return to full-time software engineering after a career break.**

Learn more about Thoughtworks Pivot here: [https://www.thoughtworks.com/en-sg/careers/thoughtworks-pivot/singapore](https://www.thoughtworks.com/en-sg/careers/thoughtworks-pivot/singapore)

**Join us for our Open House on November 13!**
Come get an insider’s look at Thoughtworks Pivot and what your next chapter in tech could look like. We'll cover:
\- Program overview\, eligibility and selection criteria
\- Sharing from Thoughtworkers leaders
\- Live Q&A session
\- Light lunch will be provided

***Note: As Thoughtworks Pivot is designed specifically to support women returning to tech after a career break,*** ***this information session is open to female attendees only. Thank you for your understanding.***
