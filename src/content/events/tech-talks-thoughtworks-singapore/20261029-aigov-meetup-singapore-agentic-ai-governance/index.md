**We are partnering with Docker to host the AIGov meetup.** **RSVP to secure your spot!**

AI agents are no longer just chat assistants. They write code, run shell commands, call tools and touch real systems on their own. That is powerful, but it also raises a hard question for every team: how do we keep these agents safe, and how do we know the rules apply to every agent in the company?

Join us for an evening of talks, live demos and good conversation with practitioners from Docker and Thoughtworks. We will look at how teams are setting guardrails for AI agents, from sandboxing and policy to data and platform governance.
Whether you are a developer, platform engineer, security lead or just curious about where agentic AI is heading, this one is for you.
