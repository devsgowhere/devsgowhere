This month, we're going into Decision Models and Agent Harnesses.

Talks:
**"Learning from Decision Models" - Martin Andrews**
After a quick introduction to the new Gemini Decision 1 model, Martin will show how specific decisions can be dynamically trained into very fast local models based on EmbeddingGemma-2.

**"Building Custom Harnesses for 2027" - Sam Witteveen**
In a this talk, Sam will talk about where Agent Harnesses are going in 2027 and how people are moving away from well known frameworks in the pursuit of vertical harnesses and agents.
