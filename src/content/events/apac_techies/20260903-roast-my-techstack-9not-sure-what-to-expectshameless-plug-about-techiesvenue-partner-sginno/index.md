​**AI can fix your code. It cannot fix a bad architecture decision.**

> ​**_Roast My TechStack_** _is a technical community demo session_ where builders put their architecture, design decisions and constraints on the table for other technical people to examine.

​There is no actual roasting. Just curious questions, technical debate and cheeky energy.

​**The goal: make better architecture decisions by learning from other builders.**

## ​How It Works

​Each presenter gets **30 minutes**.

​15 min: For presenters walk us through:

-   ​What they are building
    
-   ​Their architecture and `architecture.md` or ADRs
    
-   ​Their constraints
    
-   ​Why they chose their stack
    
-   ​The tradeoffs they made
    
-   ​What they are currently stuck on
    

​15 min: Q&A section where the audience digs into the architecture:

-   ​Why this solution over the alternatives?
    
-   ​What tradeoffs did you make?
    
-   ​What breaks first?
    
-   ​Where are the bottlenecks?
    
-   ​What would you do differently?
