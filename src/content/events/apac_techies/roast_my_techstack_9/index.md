**Roast My TechStack** is a technical community demo session where builders put their architecture, design decisions, and constraints on the table for other technical people to examine.

There is no actual roasting. Just curious questions, technical debate, and cheeky energy. The goal is to make better architecture decisions by learning from other builders.

Each presenter gets 30 minutes:

- 15 minutes to walk through what they are building, their architecture, constraints, stack decisions, tradeoffs, and current challenges.
- 15 minutes for the audience to ask questions about alternatives, tradeoffs, bottlenecks, and what they would do differently.
