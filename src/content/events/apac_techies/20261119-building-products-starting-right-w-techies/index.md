**_Business decisions shape architecture. Architecture shapes what the business can do._**

When you’re building a product, knowing just one side isn’t enough anymore.

As a solo builder, founder, PM or technical lead, you’re constantly making decisions across product, business and engineering.

**_How much should you build for the MVP? Where can you take shortcuts? And which decisions might come back to bite you later?_**

Join us and explore those tradeoffs together.

We'll take a simple product scenario and work through it from **_both the business and technical POV._** Compare approaches with others, challenge assumptions, and see how different decisions shape both the product and the architecture behind it.
