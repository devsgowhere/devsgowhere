**_What does it take to turn AI-generated animations into something you can actually ship?_**

AI is making it easier to create animations from simple prompts. But bringing motion into real products comes with its own challenges, from keeping designs consistent to making sure animations perform across different devices and platforms.

Join us for **Code Meets Animation**, a hands-on afternoon with **LottieFiles, 65Labs, Lorong AI, AI Collective Singapore, and community partner Noticebob**, exploring how AI, code, and motion come together in real design and development workflows.

Hear from the teams, pick up some practical techniques, and put them to the test in a **live Lottie AI Challenge**, where you'll create your own animations and see what others in the room come up with.

**_Curious about AI, animation, or bringing motion into digital products?_** _Whether you're a developer, designer, PM, founder, or simply someone keen to experiment with AI-generated motion, come join us! No prior animation experience needed._

---

## Here's the rundown!

**From Prompt to Motion**

> Get a first look at AI tools by LottieFiles and explore how AI-generated animations can fit into real design and development workflows.

**Building with AI**

> Hear from **CodeWithAI** on using AI as a creative and coding partner, with practical techniques you can bring into your own builds.

**Live Lottie AI Challenge**

> Put it into practice! Create animations against the clock, experiment with different approaches, and stand a chance to win prizes on the night.

**Beyond Animation Generation**

> What comes after generating an animation? Explore how motion work is changing for designers, developers, and product teams, from design consistency and performance to what it takes to ship.
