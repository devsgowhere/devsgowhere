**_What goes into building better recommender systems, and keeping AI agents from running wild?_**

Join practitioners from GoTo Group and Docker as they share two different technical perspectives, from using embeddings and knowledge graphs to improve product understanding for search and recommendations, to running coding agents safely in sandboxed environments.

---

## _More About The Sharings_

**_[Le Yi (Data Science Manager, GoTo Group)](https://sg.linkedin.com/in/le-yi-seah-38279b145?utm_source=luma)_** _&_ **_[Trieu Le (Algorithm Engineer, GoTo Group)](https://sg.linkedin.com/in/ledangtrieu?utm_source=luma)_** will share on **_“Building Product Understanding for Search & Recommendations.”_**

> Can embeddings truly understand what a product is? In large-scale marketplaces, noisy and inconsistent product data makes it challenging to build reliable search and recommendation systems.
> 
> Drawing on GoTo's work, Trieu and Le Yi will explore how **LLMs, multimodal encoders, and heterogeneous knowledge graphs** can help structure product information, connect product attributes with user behaviour, and build a more reliable semantic layer for search and recommendations. Hear about the approaches and engineering considerations behind making product information more interpretable and useful for downstream ML applications. _(Technical Level: 200)_

**_[Oleg Shelajev (Developer Relations, Docker)](https://www.linkedin.com/in/shelajev/?utm_source=luma)_** will share on **_“You're Absolutely Right, It Was Your Home Directory!”_**

> Letting AI agents loose on development tasks sounds great, until one decides to optimise your home directory or break your environment in the process. How do you give agents the freedom to work autonomously without giving them unrestricted access to your system?
> 
> Oleg will introduce Docker Sandboxes, an isolated execution environment for coding agents, and explore why basic isolation isn't always enough. Through practical examples, he'll unpack how filesystem restrictions, network controls, and secret management can help contain agent behaviour, alongside the engineering considerations behind running agents more safely. _(Technical Level: 100)_

**[Trieu Le](https://sg.linkedin.com/in/ledangtrieu?utm_source=luma)** is an Algorithm Engineer at GoTo Group, specialising in search and recommendation systems. His work focuses on building scalable semantic layers that transform noisy, multimodal data into structured knowledge for search, recommendations, and downstream ML applications. Previously, he developed AI capabilities for e-commerce at Lazada (Alibaba Group), several of which remain in production today.
