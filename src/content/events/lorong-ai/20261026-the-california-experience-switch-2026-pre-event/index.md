Join us for an evening where West Coast meets the Lion City, and we bring two of the world’s most connected innovation ecosystems to come together for one evening.

Taking place on the eve of SWITCH 2026, the California Experience is an exclusive networking reception bringing together the California delegation of the 2026 California–Singapore SWITCH Trade Mission with Singapore-based investors, government representatives, and business partners.

This is a great opportunity to engage directly with eight leading California organizations spanning universities, economic development agencies, and innovation infrastructure - and to discover the partnership, research, and investment opportunities available across one of the world's most dynamic innovation ecosystems.

The California Delegation includes:

-   **_Southern California Wine Country EDC_** \- One of only 3 accredited economic development organizations in California, fostering sustainable growth and innovation across life sciences, aerospace, and beyond
    
-   **_University of California, Riverside_** - A strategic gateway for international institutions and companies entering the U.S. market, backed by a comprehensive Soft-Landing Platform
    
-   **_San Diego State University_** \- A leading public research university driving innovation and entrepreneurship, with strong partnerships across defense, maritime, aerospace, and AI
    
-   **_Aggie Square_** \- UC Davis's new innovation district combining world-class research and clinical expertise to accelerate breakthrough technologies.
    
-   **_Murrieta Innovation Center_** \- Southern California's premier life sciences and health technology incubator, offering a soft landing for international startups
    
-   **_City of Riverside_** \- An award-winning, internationally accredited team driving FDI attraction, smart city development, and growth across advanced manufacturing, climate and energy, healthcare and biotech, aerospace and innovation-driven enterprises.
    
-   **_UC Berkeley Bakar Labs_** \- UC Berkeley's flagship incubator supporting innovation in life sciences, energy, and materials
    
-   **_Contra Costa Transportation Authority_** \- A leader in autonomous vehicle technology, operating GoMentum Station, one of the nation's largest AV proving grounds

## **Meet the Speakers**

[Emily Desai](https://www.linkedin.com/in/emily-desai/?utm_source=luma) - Chief Deputy Director

[California Governor’s Office of Business and Economic Development](https://business.ca.gov/?utm_source=luma)

Emily Desai was appointed by California Governor Gavin Newsom to serve as the Chief Deputy Director at the Governor’s Office of Business and Economic Development (GO-Biz). Under the direction of the GO-Biz Director, Emily oversees all aspects of the organization, which is responsible for the state’s economic strategy. Prior to her time at GO-Biz, Emily served the U.S. Congress as a Senior Project Manager for international affairs, trade, and national security at the U.S. Government Accountability Office where she led high-impact reports, testimonies, and briefings for Congressional leaders to act on emerging regulatory challenges. Emily was appointed by Secretary Gina Raimondo to serve on the U.S. Investment Advisory Council and appointed by Ambassador Katherine Tai to serve on the Intergovernmental Policy Advisory Committee for the Office of the U.S. Trade Representative. She is a 2024 U.S. Presidential Leadership Scholar. Emily grew up in Arizona and graduated summa cum laude and with honors from the University of Arizona. She has a Master’s in Public Policy from U.C. Berkeley’s Goldman School of Public Policy.

[George Ayache](https://www.linkedin.com/in/georgeayache/?utm_source=luma) - Senior Commercial Officer & Digital Attaché

[U.S. Embassy](https://sg.usembassy.gov/?utm_source=luma)

George Ayache currently serves as the Senior Commercial Officer and Digital Attaché at the U.S. Embassy in Singapore. In this capacity, he oversees a diverse portfolio of sectors, including information and communications technology (ICT) and digital services, financial services, franchising, design and construction, water, maritime, and land transportation infrastructure, as well as the SelectUSA program, which promotes foreign direct investment into the United States. Prior to his current appointment, George served as a Commercial Officer at the U.S. Embassy in Mexico City from July 2022 to July 2024. Before joining the U.S. Commercial Service, he spent more than five years as an International Trade Compliance Analyst in the Enforcement and Compliance unit of the U.S. Department of Commerce, specializing in antidumping and countervailing duty investigations. George holds a Bachelor of Science in Economics and French from DePaul University in Chicago, Illinois, and a Master of Arts in International Relations and Economics from Johns Hopkins University's School of Advanced International Studies (SAIS) in Washington, D.C. His academic expertise focuses on international politics and economics, and he is a native Spanish speaker.
