For our November meetup, we will have the ATLAS team (part of SEA-LION by AI Singapore) to share with us.  
  
With all the talk about Sovereign AI, and Singapore leading the way, ATLAS is set up to address two gaps for Sovereign AI to take place. They are the data gap and the local culture gap, for AI to be well-used and adapted in the various communities out there. See synopsis below for further details on the content. :)

This will be the last 2026 meetup for DataScience SG as we wrap up another successful year with your support and attendance! :)

**Title: Beyond the Code: Discovering the Right Data for AI**

**Synopsis:** Great AI starts with the right data. But how do you find datasets that are relevant, trustworthy, and suitable for your AI application?

Join AI Singapore for an interactive, hands-on workshop exploring the role of data in AI development and how open datasets can accelerate early experimentation and proof-of-concept (POC) development.

Discover how ATLAS-Data helps you explore datasets across languages and domains, then put your skills to the test through an interactive quiz and an exciting ATLAS Treasure Hunt. _Work through a real-world AI scenario, investigate datasets, and compete to become the ATLAS Champion!_

Whether you're building your first AI prototype or exploring new applications, learn how to discover, assess, and select the right data to bring your AI ideas to life.  
  
ATLAS: [https://www.atlas-data.ai/en/about](https://www.atlas-data.ai/en/about?utm_source=luma)

**Speaker Bio:**

Jessica is a Data and AI practitioner with experience across public and private sectors. She leads the Data Platforms team at AI Singapore, driving scalable platforms for AI and advanced analytics. Her expertise covers data platforms, governance, and enterprise data management, enabling AI adoption through robust data ecosystems. She has led large-scale platform modernization initiatives, bridging strategy, engineering, and AI to deliver impactful solutions.
