**_If models trained on different data and modalities can learn similar representations, what does that tell us about how AI systems understand the world?_**

As AI systems are trained across increasingly different modalities, an intriguing question has emerged: do independently trained models eventually converge towards similar ways of representing the world?

The **Platonic Representation Hypothesis** argues that they might.

For this Paper Club, we'll unpack the recent paper **_“Proving and Improving the Platonic Representation Hypothesis with JEPAs”_** and examine what its results actually tell us about shared representations across AI systems.

We'll look at the evidence behind the hypothesis, what JEPAs add to the argument, and where alternative explanations or limitations might challenge the idea that different models are converging towards a common representation of reality.

Rather than treating the paper's conclusions as settled, we'll use it as the starting point to discuss questions such as:

-   What would count as convincing evidence that different models are genuinely converging towards shared representations?
    
-   Could apparent similarities instead come from shared data, objectives, architectures or evaluation methods?
    
-   What do the JEPA results add to the original Platonic Representation Hypothesis?
    
-   If the hypothesis is directionally right, what might it mean for how we train future multimodal AI systems?
    

The discussion will be hosted by **[Sharvesh Subhash](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sharveshsubhash/?utm_source=luma)**.

## Pre-reading

📄 **Main Paper**

**_[Proving and Improving the Platonic Representation Hypothesis with JEPAs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39302?utm_source=luma)_**  
_Randall Balestriero and Yann LeCun_

The paper explores whether models trained independently across different modalities can learn aligned representations, using Joint-Embedding Predictive Architectures (JEPAs) to investigate the conditions under which this convergence might emerge.

📄 **Optional Counter-reading**

**_[Same-State Pairs Make One JEPA Proof of the Platonic Representation Hypothesis Vacuous](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16182?utm_source=luma)_**  
_Xin Li_

A recent critique that challenges assumptions behind the JEPA argument and provides another perspective for interrogating what the results actually demonstrate.

**Pre-reading is encouraged** so that you can follow and contribute better to the discussion.
