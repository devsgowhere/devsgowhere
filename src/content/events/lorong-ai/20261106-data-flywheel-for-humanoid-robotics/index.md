_How do we generate enough useful data for humanoid robots to keep learning and improving?_

Dr. Fan Shi will explore how physical simulation, digital twins, and reward modelling can help **scale** **expensive real-world data with large-scale synthetic data**, addressing one of the key challenges in humanoid robotics: generating sufficient high-quality training data for increasingly complex tasks.

Drawing from his work in robot learning for humanoid manipulation and locomotion, he'll discuss how simulation can support the development of embodied AI, and how learning in simulated environments can translate to robots operating in the physical world.

He'll also touch on what it takes to move these advances beyond research, and the opportunities and challenges in translating developments in humanoid robotics into real-world applications.
