**What's the thing you keep meaning to build when you have the time?**

Maybe it's a half-finished app. An experiment sitting in a repo. A tool you made for yourself. Something weird you started over a weekend. Or an idea you've been wanting to turn into something real.

**Side Quest is a place to actually work on it.**

Bring an AI project, experiment or idea you're working on outside your main work and spend the afternoon making progress alongside other builders at Lorong AI.

Most of the session is protected build time. Work on your own thing, see what others around you are making, and tap into the room when it's useful. Maybe someone has dealt with the problem you're stuck on. Maybe you know something that helps another builder move. Or maybe you just need a room full of people actually building to finally make some progress.

Towards the end, a few builders will show us what they've been working on. Not polished pitches or demo-day presentations. Just what they're building, what's working, what's broken and what they're trying next.

---

## How Side Quest Works

**🤔What's Your Side Quest?**

> We'll start by making everyone's Side Quest visible in the room: what are you working on today, what are you hoping to move, and where could you use a hand?

**🛠️Build**

> Then we get out of the way.
> 
> You'll have a protected block to work on your own Side Quest. Build independently, pull someone in when you're stuck, or help another builder if you spot something you know.

**🗣️Side Quest Shares**

> We'll hear from a handful of builders about what they're making.
> 
> Projects can be early, unfinished, experimental or currently broken. That's part of the point.

---

## **Important Notes**

-   **Bring a laptop and an AI project, experiment or idea you want to work on.**
    
-   You don't need a polished project. You can bring something unfinished or start something you've already been thinking about.
    
-   AI should be a meaningful part of what you're building or experimenting with.
    
-   This isn't a workshop, so there won't be a common tutorial or step-by-step build.
    
-   Come ready to work independently and help other builders where you can.
    
-   **Build, don't pitch.** Side Quest isn't a space for prospecting, recruiting or promoting products, services, courses or programmes.
    

---

## More About the Series

Side Quest is Lorong AI's regular build session for people working on AI projects outside their main work.

Bring something you're already building, an experiment you've been meaning to try or an idea you're ready to start. Spend protected time making progress alongside other builders, get unstuck with help from the room and see what others in the community are working on.

Projects don't need to be polished or finished. Side Quest is about making progress, sharing unfinished work and helping each other keep building.
