Join industry leaders, innovators, and ecosystem partners for an exclusive workshop exploring the future of robotics supply chains, manufacturing, and collaborative partnerships.

This session brings together robotics companies, manufacturers, and solution providers to share real-world experiences in navigating supply chain challenges, scaling production, and building resilient partnerships to support the growth of Singapore’s robotics ecosystem.

## More About the Speakers:

**_[Infineon Technologies](https://www.infineon.com/?utm_source=luma)_** will share on **“Building the Future Physical AI Ecosystem on Chips.”**

> Infineon Technologies is a leading semiconductor company that enables robotics through advanced power electronics, sensors, microcontrollers, and safety technologies. Its solutions help robots move precisely, navigate intelligently, operate safely, and improve energy efficiency across industrial, mobile, and humanoid robotics applications.

**_[MindOn](https://www.mindon.tech/?utm_source=luma)_** will share on **“From Robotics Technology to Real-World Deployment: MindOn’s Approach to Physical AI.”**

> MindOn develops the AI brain for robots, enabling different robot types to learn and perform tasks autonomously across real-world applications.

**_[Robospec](https://robospec.ai/?utm_source=luma)_** will share on **“From Intelligence to Action: Robospec Hero + Franka Robotics.”**

> Robospec develops physical AI that teaches robots human skills, enabling rapid deployment of intelligent automation in manufacturing environments.

## **More About RoboNexus Supply Chain Workshop Series**

The **RoboNexus Supply Chain Workshop Series** is a quarterly platform designed to support robotics startups and SMEs in navigating the complexities of supply chain design, manufacturing, and scaling.

Bringing together regional and international companies across the robotics and manufacturing value chain, the series creates a space for practitioners to exchange first-hand experiences and practical insights.

Participants can expect perspectives on:

-   Component sourcing and supplier ecosystem development
    
-   Manufacturing partnerships and production scaling
    
-   Cost optimisation and operational efficiency
    
-   Managing global supply chain and geopolitical risks
    
-   Building resilient and adaptable supply chains
    

Each session focuses on candid sharing of real-world challenges, lessons learned, and actionable strategies that participants can apply to their own operations.

Beyond knowledge exchange, RoboNexus also brings together startups, suppliers, manufacturers, and ecosystem partners to foster new connections and collaboration across Singapore’s robotics and advanced manufacturing landscape.
