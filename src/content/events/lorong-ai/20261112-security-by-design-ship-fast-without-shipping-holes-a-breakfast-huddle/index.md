Every startup wants to ship fast and reach as many people as possible — that’s the right instinct! But speed has a shadow: the auth you bolted on last-minute, the API key hardcoded “just for now,” the customer data sitting one misconfigured bucket away from the front page. Security debt is quiet until it isn’t, and by then it’s an investor walking away from diligence, a regulator asking questions, or a breach notice you have to send your users.

This workshop is about designing security from the start, not patching it in after an incident. We’ll cover how you design your product from the infrastructure level to minimize the security surface, cleanly separate risks, and implement clear controls and visibility. Our topics will include threat modeling for early-stage products, secure-by-default architecture, secrets and access management, and a pre-launch security checklist.

Bring your product. You’ll be grouped with founders at your stage — solo founder, small team, or full engineering org — and our CTOs will pressure-test it and coach you on the risks that actually apply to you. You’ll walk away with a concrete, stage-specific list of what to fix.
