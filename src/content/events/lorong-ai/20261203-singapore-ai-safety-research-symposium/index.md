**About the event**  
The Singapore AI Safety Research Symposium brings together the organisations and researchers building Singapore’s AI safety ecosystem. It convenes leading international AI safety organisations alongside Singapore-based research groups, policymakers, and AI developers to showcase frontier safety work happening in and through Singapore.  
  
The symposium also marks the completion of SASH’s inaugural three-month AI Safety Fellowship, with our fellows presenting their research.  
  
**What to expect**

-   AI Safety Research Showcase and networking lunch
    
-   Interactive talks and sessions from international and Singapore-based AI safety researchers
    
-   Curated networking with researchers, institutions, and policymakers working at the frontier of AI safety
    

**Confirmed speakers**

-   Jess Bergs, Engineering Lead at UK AI Security Institute
    
-   Lukas Struppek, Research Scientist at [FAR.AI](http://far.ai/?utm_source=luma)
    
-   Zhengyuan Liu, Team Lead at A\*STAR Centre for Frontier AI Research
    
-   Shaun Khoo, Staff Data Scientist at GovTech Singapore
    
-   Tan Zhi Xuan, Assistant Professor at NUS Department of Computer Science
    

We also have our 18 fellows from institutions including NUS, Tsinghua, IIT, Harvard, Cambridge, ETH Zürich and UC Berkeley, mentored by experts from NUS, Anthropic, Tsinghua, Fudan and others.

**Who should attend**  
This is a technical, research-focused event intended for AI safety researchers, policymakers, AI developers, academics, and representatives of research institutions with an interest in advancing AI safety research and collaboration.
