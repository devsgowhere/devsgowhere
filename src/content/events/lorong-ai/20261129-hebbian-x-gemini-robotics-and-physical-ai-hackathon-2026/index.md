# Sunday, November 29, 9:00 AM–7:00 PM SGT, in person in Singapore

📍 Lorong AI @ One-North, 69 Ayer Rajah Cres., Level 3 Vidacity, Singapore 139961

## Hosted by Hebbian Robotics (YC S26) and Google DeepMind, in partnership with Embodied AI Center (EAIC) and Lorong AI

**Sign-up deadline: November 25**

More details: [hebbianrobotics.com/hackathon](https://hebbianrobotics.com/hackathon/?utm_source=luma)

Computer Use Agents (CUA) let AI operate software. Robot Use Agents (RUA) enable autonomous work in the physical world. Be among the first to build with RUA and Gemini 4 Carbon, right here in Singapore.

Spend one day at the world's first RUA hackathon with Gemini 4 Carbon, building cutting-edge projects among the region's top robotics startups. The most creative hacks stand a chance to win cash prizes, and the robotics teams in the room will be scouting for cracked talent to join them full-time!

With RUA, you give a frontier model a goal, camera views and a set of robot tools, and it controls the robot to do the task. Teams of 4 (strictly) get one day to build the most creative robot applications they can. Every team gets an SO-101 robot arm on the day. Surprise us.

## **🏆 The Prize**

Teams will be judged by founders and members of technical staff from Google DeepMind, Y Combinator, Bifrost AI and top robotics startups in Singapore.

**Full-time Role Opportunities**: open to all participants.

## **📋 Submission Requirements**

All projects should use Hebbian's RUA SDK and Google's Gemini 4. Each team must submit the following by 5:00 PM sharp:

-   **One-Liner** for the team: describe your team's background.
    
-   **One-Paragraph** for the idea: describe the narrative behind the project.
    
-   **1-Minute Video Link**: a demo video uploaded to YouTube as a public video.
    
-   **Code Repository**: a GitHub link.
    

Submissions that don't meet the above format may be voided.

## **📊 Judging Criteria**

-   **Innovation & Creativity**: How did you work around the constraints?
    
-   **Reliability**: Does it actually work?
    
-   **X Factor**: Did you go above and beyond?
    
-   **Style**: Did the room gasp?
    

## **🎯 Competition Format**

-   **Finals:** The top 5 teams advance to live presentations of 5 minutes per team (3-min pitch + 2-min Q&A) on stage before the full judging panel of founders and special guests. Winners are announced at 7:00 PM.
