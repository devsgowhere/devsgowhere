AI applications are only as useful as the context they can access. Join us for an evening exploring how knowledge graphs and GraphRAG help developers build AI systems that retrieve more relevant information, reason over connected data, and produce answers that are easier to explain and trust.

You’ll explore:

* How to model fragmented enterprise data as connected nodes and relationships
* Why GraphRAG can add structure, precision, and explainability beyond vector-only retrieval
* How a knowledge layer connects your data to AI applications and agent workflows
* Practical patterns you can take back to your own AI, data, and developer projects

Whether you are building RAG applications, experimenting with AI agents, working with data platforms, or just getting started with graphs, you’ll leave with concrete ideas for making AI more context-aware.

Come for the technical learning, stay to connect with developers, data practitioners, and AI builders across Singapore.

We are organizing this event together with our partner IMDA ([https://www.imda.gov.sg](https://www.imda.gov.sg)). Registration information will be shared with IMDA.
