📅 Friday, 27 November 2026  
🕕 6:30 PM – 8:30 PM (doors open at 6:00 PM)  
📍 Red Hat Office, Singapore

This is bonus event of our 2026 DevOps Journey series. AI agents are leaving the laptop. This session looks at best practices for building **Secure** and **Scalable** Agentic Systems; from architecture to deployment. AI agents are moving beyond chat interfaces and coding agents that run on developer laptops with broad access to company systems.

## 🎯 What to Expect

-   🔧 Two concrete patterns for enterprise agents (not just demos on a laptop)
    
-   🤖 How durable workflows keep ephemeral agents bounded, auditable, and interruptible
    
-   💡 Discussion on accountability when agents have identity and permissions
    
-   🤝 End-of-year networking with the DevOps Singapore community

**Vincent** - Platform engineer. Has been building and managing Kubernetes clusters on AWS since 2016. Former organiser of the Docker and Kubernetes meetups in Singapore and Vietnam. Core maintainer of the community fork of CDK Terrain.

**AI Adoption Patterns for Enterprises**

Agents are moving past chat and laptop-based coding tools with broad company access. Vincent will walk through two patterns:

-   Personal Digital Agents — persistent agents with their own identity and permissions, while the employee remains accountable
    
-   Ephemeral agents orchestrated by durable workflows — event- or schedule-triggered, bounded, and reliant on the platform for state, access control, and audit
