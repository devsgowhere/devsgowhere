**ABSTRACTS**

**OT 1-2: Inside the OT Cyber Battlefield – Think Like an Attacker. Defend Like a Practitioner.**

What happens when a cyberattack moves beyond IT systems and targets the very systems that keep our industries running?

From power generation and water treatment to manufacturing and critical infrastructure, Operational Technology (OT) environments are increasingly exposed to sophisticated cyber threats. Yet, defending these environments requires more than conventional cybersecurity knowledge. It requires understanding how industrial systems communicate, how attackers exploit them, and most importantly, how to detect and defend against attacks before they disrupt operations.

Join us for an exciting, hands-on showcase of OT 1-2: Defender for OT Cybersecurity, where we take you beyond theory and into the realities of OT cyber defence.

Through a practical demonstration using an OT cyber range, participants will get a closer look at how cyber adversaries conduct reconnaissance, move laterally across industrial networks, exploit OT protocols and potentially compromise critical operational systems. More importantly, discover how defenders can analyse network traffic, identify suspicious activities, understand attacker techniques through the MITRE ATT&CK for ICS framework, and strengthen their defensive capabilities.

This session brings together three practitioners who will share different perspectives on OT cybersecurity:

-   Matthias: Bringing the strategic and operational perspectives of OT cybersecurity, industrial threat landscapes and the protection of critical infrastructure.
    
-   Ben: Sharing how an attacker could potential gain access to a power plant
    
-   Jonathan: Sharing practical industry perspectives and insights into securing industrial environments.
    

For those who enrol in the full OT 1-2 course, the experience goes even further: participants will have the opportunity to get hands-on with the simulated power plant themselves, carrying out practical attack-and-defence exercises in a controlled cyber range environment. The digital system will be showcased during the event.

**Squidbleed: Hacking in the post-Mythos era**

Squid Proxy is one of the most widely deployed web proxies, used by schools, offices and public networks.

In this talk, I present Squidbleed (CVE-2026-47729), a decades-old bug in a little-used corner of Squid that I found with Claude Mythos Preview. I'll walk through how it works, and show how an attacker can use it to steal other users' credentials.

Finally, I'll look at how advanced AI models such as Claude Mythos are changing the way bugs like this are found.
