Brought to you by Div0's AI Security Quarter (AISQ), together with C4AIL, Relw.AI and ARCS.

**ABSTRACT**  
Anyone can now build an app, an automation or an AI agent in an afternoon. But how do you make sure what you build is actually safe to use?

Join us for a hands-on, 2-hour evening workshop with **Ethan Seow**, where we'll explore how to build something small with AI while applying cybersecurity fundamentals from day one.

We'll focus on three essentials:

-   **Access:** Who can get in, and what are they allowed to do?
    
-   **Data:** What information does your app or agent access, store or share?
    
-   **Checks:** What happens when AI makes a mistake, and how do you keep things under control?
    

You'll also have the chance to hear what others in the room have been building and share your own AI projects.

**No coding experience needed.** Just bring your curiosity, a laptop and your preferred AI tool.
