## How do you build an AI agent you can trust?

Brought to you by Div0's AI Security Quarter (AISQ), together with C4AIL, Relw.AI and ARCS.

**ABSTRACT**  
An AI agent doesn't just answer - it acts. It reads your inbox, runs a check, files a ticket, calls an API. That's where the value is, and it's where the risk is. In this hands-on evening, Ethan Seow (Div0 AI Security Quarter, C4AIL, Relw.AI) builds a small agent with you and bounds it from the first step: what it can reach, what it must never do, when it hands back to you, and how you see and undo what it did. We'll set up the files your AI reads every time it starts - [CLAUDE.md](https://claude.md/?utm_source=luma) or [AGENTS.md](https://agents.md/?utm_source=luma), skills and agents - and look at the harness experienced builders put around them. Came on 24 Nov? Bring your project and take it a version further. New? Start one on the night.

**No coding experience needed.**

**SET UP INSTRUCTIONS**

1.  A laptop with your AI tool signed in - Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Codex, or a local model
    
2.  If you already build with Claude Code, Codex or Cursor, have it installed and working
    
3.  Your one-page brief and v1 from 24 Nov (if you came)\*\*
    
4.  One task you'd hand to an agent if you could trust it
