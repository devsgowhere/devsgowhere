We're planning the first Omarchy Meetup in Singapore.

This will be a hands-on community gathering for people who use Omarchy, are curious about Linux, or enjoy making their computers more personal, beautiful, and programmable.

The event will feature short Lightning Talks, an extended Omarchy installation and configuration workshop, and plenty of time to meet other developers and hardware enthusiasts.

DHH will deliver the opening talk via livestream, followed by two talks on Docker Sandboxes on Omarchy by Michael Yuan, Docker Captain.

You can join whether you are completely new to Omarchy, already have a customized setup, or simply want to see how other people work.

## Lightning Talk Speakers

- **Varun Bakshi**, Executive Director, Goldman Sachs: "Omarchy at Home, Windows at Work, SUPER+2 Apart"
- **Dragon**, Agent Engineer, ByteFuture: "Omarchy Remote"
- **Jia Huang**, Senior Research Scientist in Artificial Intelligence at A\*STAR, Principal Technical Advisor at Singularity Intelligence Institute, and Co-founder of the ADPS community: "From Coding Agents to Research Systems: Designing Reliable Agent Workflows"

## What to expect

- 5-minute Lightning Talks from community members
- Omarchy installation and setup help
- Troubleshooting for existing installations
- Desktop, terminal, dotfiles, and workflow show-and-tell
- AI coding agent and developer workflow discussions
- Keyboards, programmable hardware, and personal computing
- Informal networking with the local Linux and open source community

No previous Omarchy experience is required. Bring your laptop if you would like to install or configure Omarchy during the workshop.

If you would like to attend, share your setup, give a Lightning Talk, or help others with installation and troubleshooting, please register on Luma.
