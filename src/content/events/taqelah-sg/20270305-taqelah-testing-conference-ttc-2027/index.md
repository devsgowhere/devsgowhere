Singapore’s first grassroots testing conference is here! Join taqelah! SG for Taqelah Testing Conference (TTC) 2027 on March 5 at Matrix, Biopolis Drive. Come ready to swap testing stories, pick up fresh ideas, and meet the people who care about making software better.

Expect a day of curious conversations, practical takeaways, and plenty of taqelah! energy. Whether testing is your day job or something you’re just getting into, there’s a place for you at TTC.
